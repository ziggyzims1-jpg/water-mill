**Lessons for the graph**
- A subagent transcript is never finalised: ~63% of its assistant messages have no line with a stop_reason and keep the first streamed chunk's output_tokens; nothing on disk holds the true total.
- A top-level session transcript has the final usage on every line of a message, so max-per-message.id is the true output total there.
- The crew-vs-single-Claude token/cost comparison is biased in the crew's favour by measurement unless floor runs are excluded (agents compare does this by default now).
- cost-state outputTokens covers a session including its subagents but cannot be split per agent.
